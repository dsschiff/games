## Prove the advertised activity first

Selected K2 supports active avatar movement beside an incoming enemy road,
nearby dropped coins, priced build pads, constructed firing towers and further
investment. Independent playback shows wallet0→3→5, the5-pad construction and
then a second tower near the15 pad. Exact collection/debit/input formulas are
unproven, and Kingshot attribution is qualified by the third-party index. Keep
those boundaries in the implementation comparison.

The first checkpoint must visibly complete:

1. Move the avatar through a close, angled overhead3D battlefield.
2. Collect conspicuous nearby coins and show the wallet change.
3. Fund a marked pad with an explicit, readable original cost/control rule.
4. Construct a tower that visibly fires into the advancing column.
5. Collect battle rewards and make a second investment with a clear effect.
6. Reach a natural defended-wave win and a leak-driven loss; use actual Retry
   while preserving earned progress coherently.

Keep the avatar, two build choices and approaching road readable together.
Show coins, prices, targeting/bolts and refuge integrity clearly. Use floating
thumb movement and a contextual build action; avoid accidental spending. At a
360dp cover width, touch targets must reach48dp. Preserve state on resize and
provide sound/reduced effects, pause, checked local saves and export/import.

Avatar shooting, dash, mounting and hero death are deferred original options,
not proven reference requirements or first-loop gates. A stationary tower board
or settlement/menu timer fails this selected activity. No ads, purchases,
accounts, servers, energy gates, construction waits or forced resource grind.

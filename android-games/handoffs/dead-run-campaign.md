## A complete road campaign

Deliver 0.2.0/code2 with a substantial evacuation arc: approximately 24 stages
across four distinct districts, three weapon families, six enemy roles, four
behaviorally different bosses, a conclusive evacuation and deterministic seeded
replay. Refine these candidate counts when doing so improves substantive play;
explain any reduction before calling the full milestone complete. Aim for a
meaningful 30–60-minute first campaign, without compulsory replay, income grind,
idle timers, ads, purchases, accounts, servers or energy limits.

Keep the lower-quarter squad, portrait elevated forward road, proportional
one-finger lateral drag, forward fire, shot-changing numbered gates, conspicuous
recruits joining, harmful crossings and incoming infected throughout early,
middle and final play. Large sustained side targets must also appear during
ordinary road pressure. An arena joystick or shelter screen does not deliver
the selected activity. Use original rescue crews, transit districts and infected.

Replace the prototype's one prescribed successful lane schedule with readable
decisions. Positive gates need opportunity costs, and aiming sideways should
leave a visible forward threat less suppressed. Weapon sidegrades should change
coverage, piercing, group pressure or target timing rather than simply multiply
damage. Introduce shield/fast/group threats and road constraints with sufficient
warning; bosses need distinct telegraphs and counters. Announce enough for a
player to act without a policy reading hidden state. Do not make every beneficial
gate compulsory or every late stage the same route with larger HP.

Demonstrate at least two viable lane/target priorities on the same late-stage
definitions, a diagnosable losing choice corrected through actual Retry, and
meaningfully different weapon choices. Keep formation width legible before any
narrow obstacle: recruits currently animate outside the advertised gold contact
footprint while bullets/collisions use their settled offsets. Align the cues or
clearly distinguish noncolliding arrival ghosts. Improve crowd density and usable
camera composition while preserving advance warning and phone readability.

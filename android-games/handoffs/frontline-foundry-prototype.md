## First prove the reinforcement loop

The selected T3 creative supports a moving commander, firing near a sandbag
front, coins and priced base pads, weapon-request queues, armed recruits
joining the adjacent fight, and heavy/vehicle escalation in one connected world.
T4/T5 corroborate equipment-to-front continuity but do not prove identical free
commander control. The Top General end card's mapping to Top Force remains
qualified third-party attribution. Exact input, pickup, debit, production, unit
cap, targeting and death rules are original choices, not measured reference rules.

The first checkpoint must visibly complete:

1. Move a commander between a compact base and an adjacent threatened front in
   a close, angled overhead portrait 3D composition.
2. Fight a nearby threat and collect conspicuous currency, with readable health,
   wallet, station prices, troop requests and HQ integrity.
3. Invest in a marked equipment station using an explicit contextual action.
4. Show a waiting recruit receiving a visible weapon, leaving the queue,
   deploying and changing the adjacent fight through its own attacks.
5. Make a second consequential investment and introduce one vehicle escalation.
6. Reach natural objective victory, HQ/commander defeat and actual immediate
   Retry with coherent progress and settings preservation.

The defining causal check is station investment -> equipped recruit -> deployment
-> changed combat outcome. An abstract stat bonus or decorative queue cannot
substitute for this sequence. Keep combat, travel and investment in one world.
Original bootstrap resources/starter defense must prevent a zero-money deadlock.
Do not rely on compulsory waiting, offline production or grinding to restart.

Use floating thumb movement, automatic nearby fire if adopted and an explicit
contextual station action. At 360dp cover width, controls must reach48dp. Avoid
accidental purchases. Three fronts, ammunition logistics, medics, escort carts,
manual aim/dodge and blueprint dragging are deferred until this loop is reviewed.
Do not spend the first milestone implementing that speculative breadth.

## Reliability and review checkpoint

Provide pause, sound/reduced effects, versioned checked local saves and portable
export/import. Disable native Back-to-quit explicitly. TextEdit must receive real
touch focus; Export copies directly with honest feedback, Paste restores and
invalid Import preserves all progress. If touch-to-mouse emulation is enabled,
filter its mouse copies from world movement so one finger causes one action.
Keep active state stable across resize and background/cold restoration.

Test station cost/occupancy, queue ownership, equipment consumption, deployment
cap, targeting, unique reward collection, objective/leaks, Retry and failed
save/import recovery. Units must not become permanently stranded at gates.
Use isolated desktop APPDATA. Actual engine ScreenTouch/GUI events and normal
processing must establish the playthrough; state-reading input policies are
allowed. Progress/economy injection or forced terminal redraws are unit fixtures
only and cannot prove a playable route.

Freeze a scoped commit with exact executed source and artifact hashes, raw inputs,
app-written states, logs, readable PNGs and a short continuous original-game movie
showing the complete reinforcement cycle, vehicle escalation and natural outcomes.
No physical phone FPS, finger, folding, sound or human fun claims from desktop
capture. Stop at that committed checkpoint for independent tests and Astra
appeal/fidelity review before broad campaign expansion.

ending and replay: the brief proposes12missions,4unit roles,3support buildings,

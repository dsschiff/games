## Comparison decision

Both observed sources sell moving a growing character through enemy zones, close combat and upgrades. B3 emphasizes compact power-gated pens and very fast visible growth; B4 shows a broader portrait exploration world with recovery and timers. We cannot yet conclude the paid ads accurately represent sustained core play. Preserve Daniel's explicitly requested hook → pull → defeat → absorb → grow activity as the main loop, and retain the official creative's close overhead 3D visual character. Make pen/gate objectives part of authored exploration rather than claiming their exact reference behavior is established.

## First prototype boundary after interpretation review

Prove **move → visible hook/tether and pull → defeat/consume → conspicuous size or power growth → stronger gated encounter** with win/loss/retry. Start with the visible movement stick and a simple contextual/automatic hook; do not make unverified twin-stick aim the defining interaction. Preserve tangible growth as a reward, not only sidegrade choice. Official developer promotion is an acceptable selected promotional reference; missing proof of a paid placement does not indefinitely block this prototype.

Manual aim, dodge, pulling the player toward terrain/heavy targets, tension, explosive interactions and limited trait slots below are **deferred original ideas**, pending an observed continuous hook passage and a satisfying first prototype. They must not replace the proven growth loop. Encounter counts and timings below are planning candidates, not fixed quotas before appeal testing.

## Candidate full-game design and deferred ideas

- **Camera:** portrait, angled overhead 3D orthographic-follow view, player near center; a little look-ahead toward aim direction. Terrain, cliffs, fenced arenas and large enemies read clearly. Original salvage diver with magnetic winch and crystalline creatures; use no copied orange butcher design, monster mesh, layout or UI.
- **Controls:** left floating thumbstick moves; right drag aims tether with range/obstruction preview, release fires. Tap right button optionally auto-aims nearest valid target. Hook pulls one light enemy toward player, or pulls player toward heavy anchored enemies/terrain. Clear cooldown ring. Auto-strike when close, with a timed dodge button; aim/pull remains active throughout campaign. This manual control is a design choice pending reference review.
- **Core loop:** scout a small encounter, isolate a target with hook, dodge its allies, defeat and absorb a clearly marked trait, choose an upgrade, then open a short route or challenge a larger target. Defeat feels quick for ordinary enemies, 2–5 seconds; elites 15–30 seconds, bosses 60–90 seconds. Targets require playtesting, not source measurements.
- **Depth:** target types grant armor/speed/force crystals; limited active trait slots make specialization meaningful. Armor-heavy hooks resist stagger but reduce mobility; speed builds can flank shields but lose durability; force builds reel heavier targets. Hooks break on solid obstacles; tension and enemy wind-up are legible. Use enemies as temporary shields or pull explosive pods into groups. No unconditional permanent snowball that makes placement/aim irrelevant.
- **Progression:** candidate 4 compact islands, 12 encounters and 4 distinct bosses, 6 enemy roles, 3 tether modes, roughly 40–60 minutes. Absorbing enemies must visibly grow size/power and enable a stronger encounter; supplement that growth with tactical unlocks rather than replacing it. A zone may gate on a demonstrated tool use or boss token, never a long numerical grind. Clearing an island creates an immediate optional challenge variant rather than waiting for enemies to respawn. Active build choices can be reset freely.
- **Pacing:** playable hook within 15 seconds; first absorption before minute one; regular decisions every 5–15 seconds. Healing fountains replenish immediately at safe checkpoints; no AFK requirements, daily gates or respawn timers. Death returns to last encounter with build intact; retry immediately.
- **Replay:** arena seeds, limited-trait challenges, boss time/precision medals and alternate tether loadouts. Campaign has a conclusive final island and ending, with replay unlocked afterward.

## Acceptance

1. First/middle/final footage shows move, aim, visible cable contact, physical pull, close combat, trait absorption and a build consequence. A generic auto-attacking survivor with decorative rope fails.
2. Demonstrate miss/obstruction, light-versus-heavy pull, cooldown, enemy wind-up/dodge, explosive interaction, three viable builds and a boss counter. Camera must preserve aiming space without hiding targets behind thumb UI.
3. Test collision/pull bounds, hook target priority, trait caps, cooldowns, checkpoint reset and save restoration; no path outside nav/collision bounds after repeated pulls.
4. Versioned saves, export/import, offline Android win/loss/retry, interrupted-save recovery, background/resume and update compatibility; reduced effects/sound settings. Visual review against B3/B4 and measured phone frame pacing remain required.

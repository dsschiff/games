## Decision and comparison

Make **steer a squad along a forward road, shoot zombies and numbered obstacles, recruit strength, defeat a large end threat** the complete game. Ad and core samples overlap in road shooting, while sustained L4 also includes shelter progression and speed-up items. Preserve road action for the full arc; do not require shelter timers or alliances to unlock it. Last Z confirms a useful comparison genre and camera, not an alternate identity or a seventh app.

## Original implementation brief

The first prototype should prove lateral squad steering → continuous forward bullets → a changing numbered obstacle/recruit outcome → zombie pressure, with a large sustained-fire side target visible during ordinary road action as in L2, plus win/loss/retry. Do not relegate every large threat to an end boss. Establish whether that feels satisfying before committing to campaign-size candidates. Side-shot shields, weapon families and weak-point systems are deferred original ideas, conditional on later review.

- **Camera/style:** portrait elevated 3D forward view, gently foreshortened road, squad held in lower quarter, incoming decisions readable at least 2 seconds ahead. Original municipal rescue crew, reclaimed transit bridge, scrap machines and fungal infected. Preserve densely visible crowd pressure with strong silhouettes; avoid copying the advertised white armored figure, gun meshes, character uniforms or level.
- **Input:** one-finger horizontal drag with proportional movement, automatic forward shooting, automatic forward scroll. No joystick arena movement. Optional tap skill added only after core loop review. Clamp steering to safe road bounds and show squad footprint so a wide formation's hazard contact is predictable.
- **Base rules:** ordinary zombies advance toward squad; bullet hits damage them. Numbered barriers require sustained fire and reveal recruit pickups when destroyed; choose between fewer near recruits and a tougher high-reward target farther away. Some gates begin negative and bullets increment them; crossing applies the shown result. These are explicit original rules pending close source verification, not certified original arithmetic.
- **Meaningful depth after prototype:** limited fire angle/width, split-lane opportunity costs, recruit formation width against road obstacles, shielded zombies requiring side shots, piercing versus spread weapons and boss weak spots with readable wind-ups. Targeting one gate leaves a horde less suppressed; acquiring more troops increases firepower but makes a formation harder to steer through gaps. Avoid filling the road with compulsory good gates and calling that strategy.
- **Progression candidates:** 24 road stages across 4 districts, 3 weapon families, 6 enemy roles and 4 bosses; 40–55-minute first clear. Early stages 45–75 seconds, late stages 90–150 seconds. Unlock equipment choices from wins, not repeated numerical grind. Include a conclusive evacuation finale. Counts and pacing are candidates to validate, not fixed content quotas before prototype appeal.
- **Loss/retry:** squad depleted or final threat reaches the defended line loses; finish objective wins. Immediate restart with clear collision/gate recap. Survival decisions should determine outcome; no hidden mandatory ad revive or forced failure.
- **Replay:** seeded road layouts, optional rescue/no-loss medals and endless evacuation after campaign. Persistent unlocks are bounded sidegrades plus stage progression; in-run squad growth remains conspicuous. No daily check-in, energy or real-time construction.
- **Feedback:** bullets clearly contact selected targets, gate changes animate once per hit group, recruit joins visible at readable speed, boss health and danger telegraphs remain clear. Reduced gore/effects and independent sound control; original audio because reference audio was muted.

## Acceptance

1. Early/middle/final footage still uses L2's road/squad/numbered-threat activity and forward portrait camera. Generic twin-stick arena survival or a shelter management game fails this selected target.
2. Demonstrate lateral input, bullet-to-number change, recruit/formation growth, negative outcome, horde contact, different successful lane choices, final threat, win/loss/retry and later progression. No unavoidable collision due to camera or hidden formation width.
3. Rules tested with fixed seeds: bullet targeting/hit counts, gate arithmetic and crossing once only, recruit cap/formation, obstacle collision, boss outcome, retry restoration and safe save resumption. No gate can grant duplicated rewards after import/resume.
4. Offline Android lifecycle, versioned atomic saves, export/import and update preservation, readable phone touch UI, sound/reduced effects and measured heavy-horde performance; independent visual comparison and physical-phone fun review remain separate from automated checks.

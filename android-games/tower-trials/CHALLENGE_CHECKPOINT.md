## Play

Open **Settings → Seeded Challenges**. Choose Keys, Shell/sun, Rescue fork or Woven finale; enter a seed0–2147483647, choose weave0–3 and treasure on/off. New advances the seed. Retry, Undo and Replay retain the exact active puzzle. Campaign returns to its prior action history, earned profile and settings. Records shows the256 most recently completed challenge records, with separate rescue/par/treasure/clean medals and version/seed/options replay.

The four small structural grammars vary prerequisites, key exchange/placement, shield budgets, irreversible commitments, rescue/treasure timing, multiplier timing and ordered enemies. Layout and arithmetic also vary. They do not promise infinitely many unique topologies. The512-case matrix produced31 serialized structural signatures after excluding enemy strengths and room placement; this is not a graph-isomorphism or uniqueness proof.

## Generation and save boundaries

- At most4 normal candidate attempts; each BFS is capped at1024 popped nodes and4096 queued nodes. Search exhaustion is distinct from proving a losing commitment. All accepted puzzles have a shortest win witness, a treasure/medal witness where offered and a combat-safe commitment with no winning continuation. A separate route reaches actual defeat after that commitment.
- If attempts are exhausted, a tested family-specific fixed backup is independently validated. Its metadata retains the requested seed/options and marks the backup explicitly; it is not represented as another random layout. All four forced-budget fallback paths pass.
- Generated content has a separate kind and generator version/seed/options, a full saved puzzle snapshot, separate action/Undo history and separate medal records. Active restore/Undo/Retry/Replay never regenerate it. Campaign state remains stored independently.
- Import validates exact snapshot fields, IDs/references, distinct2×4 room cells,4–8rooms,1–2ordered enemies per room, integer/resource bounds, texts, routes, options, goal/keeper/treasure consistency and bounded win/medal/losing witnesses. Record counts and search budgets are bounded; completed active runs require a matching medal record. No unchecked imported state is applied before a successful save commit.
- Three compatibility sources are distinguished: the original touch-created code1 save; a touch-created code2 active route with18earned clears; and a touch-created active generated save. A separate synthetic forced-backup fixture deliberately differs from normal generation for its seed/options. Tests prove snapshot restore makes zero generator calls and preserves that difference. These are desktop fixtures, not APK-update evidence.

Two authored-review follow-ups are also fixed: cold-restored defeat rebuilds its visible last-action explanation, and later route-specific hints require an explicit tap. Factual rules remain visible. Original catalog bytes are untouched.

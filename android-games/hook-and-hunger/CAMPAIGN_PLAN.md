Candidate arc: restore four harbor beacons, then sail home. Each compact island has a readable safe dock/forge, two positional encounter problems and a distinct boss. Twelve encounters/four bosses remain provisional; trim or revise them if appeal/playtesting supports a better arc. No repeated resource grind or inflated-HP substitute for useful decisions.

The three paid weapon steps must all increase meaningful capability and be visible on the diver. Optional replay loadout choices can trade reach/cleave/recovery after those purchases; they cannot replace the required power progression. Enemy stats/rosters/rewards, collision geometry and boss schedules should become explicit data as this arc expands.

For each gate: sum all guaranteed unique rewards before it; purchase cost must be comfortably below that sum, and safe checkpoints cannot consume finite progression currency. Never debit an unavailable purchase or award the same fixed ID twice. A failed encounter preserves all earned permanent progression and wallet; surviving enemies reset coherently. Test each stage from a fresh island checkpoint and from a wounded mid-encounter retry, including purchased-but-not-cleared saves.

Replay candidate: immediate seeded arena variants, loadout comparison and efficiency medals based on time/hits/recoveries. Campaign clears unlock these without waiting or replay fees. A seed changes formations/cover within validated navigable regions; it cannot change a saved active encounter after an app update. Keep the active encounter definition/version pinned in a later save schema when data becomes configurable.

Before claiming full-campaign duration or fun, measure uninterrupted human routes and purposeful decisions. Automated policy timings are verification evidence only. First/middle/final motion must show normal input-driven actions and terminals at intended tempo. Android lifecycle/update and actual Fold7 folding/performance remain separate coordinator acceptance lanes.

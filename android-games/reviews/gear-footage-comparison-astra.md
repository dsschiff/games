## Required direction for the next milestone

1. Let the scene dominate. Reference combat gives most space to an open illustrated battlefield, with compact wall/gears below. Collapse secondary utility controls, reduce permanent explanation panels, and expand terrain/troop space. Preparation may expand the board/shop, as observed in G3.
2. Make gears read as meshed machinery. Larger touching toothed wheels and recognizable generator roles/portraits should replace the square-node appearance. Retain clear power feedback, readable powered motion and accessible drag targets independent of the drawn teeth.
3. Make real production visible. G3 troops occupy the space from generator to wall and pass through an arch. Show actual generated troops emerging/marching to their exits before battlefield engagement. Timing must agree with production; do not add phantom troops merely to decorate rate labels.
4. Illustrate character and place. Original brass automata, beetles and foundries fit the direction but need strong outlined silhouettes, weapons/heads, terrain, wall arches and environmental detail. Current dark rectangles, repeated lane stripes and tiny figures remain prototype-like.
5. Make abundance dynamic and readable. Improve crowd spacing, movement variation and distinct role silhouettes. A120-unit cap is permissible; this bounded footage establishes no required unit count or production formula. Preserve performance evidence while improving art.
6. Fix the clipped bottom instruction visible in the Retry capture; check actual cover/unfolded layouts and safe areas.

## Observations and permissible original design

G3 preparation shop/Battle with cream1 was followed by blue2 and active production. The exact contact gesture, merge arithmetic and manual gate-selection rule were not isolated. Explicit preparation and meaningful changes between waves are supported; active placement every10-20seconds remains an original playtest hypothesis.

The precise power formula, explicit merge confirmation, undo, fixed budgets, counters, repair costs, seed challenges and campaign structure are permissible original choices. Negative modifiers, multiple cores and late spells are not mandatory from this bounded pass.

Next compare early, developed middle and final crowded encounters in motion. Each should show **powered gears → identifiable generated troops → wall exits → broad-field combat**, with preparation and battle readable without large explanatory panels. Final fidelity remains open until that implementation is reviewed.
